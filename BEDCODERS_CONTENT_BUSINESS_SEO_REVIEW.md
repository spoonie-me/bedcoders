# BEDCODERS — CONTENT, BUSINESS & SEO REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-08-04
**Scope:** All public-facing copy (`src/pages/**`, `index.html`), monetization surface (`backend/prisma/schema.prisma`, `backend/src/routes/checkout.ts`, `backend/src/lib/stripe.ts`, `src/pages/Pricing.tsx`), and technical SEO (`src/components/SEO.tsx`, `public/`, `vercel.json`)
**Method:** Direct read of source. Marketing claims cross-checked against `backend/prisma/seed-data/` (counted programmatically) and against the access-control code that actually enforces them.
**Companion doc:** `BEDCODERS_SPONSOR_COMMUNITY_AUDIT.md` (2026-08-02) — sponsor/community gaps are covered there and are **not** re-derived here.
**Read-only:** No code was changed.

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

1. **`vercel.json` returns a hard 404 for the entire public marketing site.** The route table's final rule is `{ "src": "/(.*)", "status": 404, "dest": "/404.html" }` (`vercel.json:59`), and the SPA-rewrite list above it omits `/pricing`, `/blog`, `/blog/*`, `/for-teams`, `/signup`, `/login`, `/welcome`, `/share-story`, and every legal page. Those are 15 of the 18 URLs in `public/sitemap.xml`, including the two highest-priority ones. `public/404.html` does not exist either. **This is the single highest-impact finding in the document** — every other SEO and funnel observation is downstream of it.

2. **The platform has no working analytics.** GA4 ships with the literal placeholder `G-XXXXXXXXXX` (`index.html:292,297`) and Umami with `YOUR-UMAMI-WEBSITE-ID` (`index.html:308`). PostHog is named in the Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy but its SDK is loaded nowhere in `src/`. Every `trackEvent('pricing_view')` / `checkout_start` call fires into a no-op. There is **zero** funnel data, so no pricing or conversion decision made now can be evidence-based.

3. **Marketing numbers are wrong in both directions, and contradict each other.** Landing claims "840+ practice exercises" (`Landing.tsx:125`); Pricing claims "68+ lessons / 60+ exercises" (`Pricing.tsx:105-106`). Actual seeded content is **146 lessons and 356 exercises across all 4 tracks**. One page overclaims by 2.4×; the other undersells by 2.1×/5.9×.

4. **The free tier is ~24× larger than advertised.** Copy promises "one full lesson" (`Pricing.tsx:77`, `Landing.tsx:115`, `_BlogLayout.tsx:90`). `backend/src/routes/lessons.ts:46-49` actually grants every module with `order === 1`, and there are 24 such modules (one per domain, across 24 domains) — roughly a fifth of the curriculum, free, forever.

5. **The annual plan's value prop is halved in the copy.** €120/yr vs €144 = **€24 saved / 2 months free**, which is what `stripe.ts:28` and `.env.example` say. The Pricing card says "Save 2 months" in the subtitle (`Pricing.tsx:123`) and "1 month free (save €12)" in the feature list (`Pricing.tsx:133`) — on the same card. Landing repeats the wrong number (`Landing.tsx:158`).

6. **"Pause the subscription" is promised three times and implemented zero times.** `Pricing.tsx:60`, the FAQ JSON-LD in `index.html:259`, and the general "crash-day kind" positioning all commit to it. `schema.prisma:90-105` has no pause field, `checkout.ts` has no `pause_collection` call, and the only churn exit is the Stripe billing portal. For an audience whose defining trait is unpredictable capacity, this is both the most important retention lever and an unbacked consumer claim now published in a rich result.

7. **The reversed Spooniversity merger left live wiring, not just stale docs.** `subscribe.ts:17` defaults every Bedcoders email signup to the `spooniversity_launch` sequence, which sends from `roi@spooniversity.com` and links to `spooniversity.com` (`email-sequences.ts:19,27`). A `bedcoders_welcome` sequence exists (`email-sequences.ts:127`) and is never the default. The 3,721 imported Substack subscribers are auto-enrolled into the Spooniversity sequence (`email-sequences.ts:163`).

8. **Content mandates are violated on the one page that most needs them.** `ShareStory.tsx` requires a real name (`:91`), a "Current role" (`:95`), and track *completion* (`:101`) — a shame-architecture and pseudonymity violation against `docs/bedcoders-dev-brief.md` §6 items 2 and 5, on the page where chronically ill users are asked to publish something about themselves.

9. **Good news, so it doesn't get lost:** `index.html` structured data, `robots.txt`, `public/sitemap.xml`, and `src/components/SEO.tsx` are genuinely above-average work — Organization + WebSite + Course ItemList + FAQPage JSON-LD, per-page canonicals with no duplicates, correct `noIndex` on app/ad pages, a considered AI-crawler policy, and a `<noscript>` fallback. The SEO *foundation* is strong; it is being wasted by items 1 and 3.

10. **The repo was not renamed.** `git remote -v` resolves to `github.com/spoonie-me/bedcoders`, and every production URL in `.env.example`, `vercel.json`, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, and `SEO.tsx` is `bedcoders.com`. There is **no live domain change and no redirect work required.** The `medinformics.com` inconsistency is confined to `docs/bedcoders-dev-brief.md` (see C-12).

### Blog content quality

Four posts, all in `src/pages/blog/`, all hand-built React with correct `BlogPosting` + `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD via `_BlogLayout.tsx`. Quality of the writing is high and on-voice — `CodingWithChronicIllness.tsx` in particular is the single best piece of positioning on the site and is correctly keyword-loaded (`:14`).

Weaknesses: the posts are **thin** (~700–1,100 words) for the head terms they target; there is **no internal linking** between posts and none into the curriculum (`_BlogLayout.tsx` offers only a signup CTA and a "back to blog" link); there are **no images anywhere** on the site, so no image-search surface and no visual proof; and there has been **no new post since February 2026**, against a stated twice-weekly cadence. Three of four posts target generic AI topics ("What Is AI Literacy", "Prompt Engineering in 2026") where Bedcoders has no ranking advantage against enormous incumbents; only one targets the defensible niche. See S-13.
